Add your phone data

TikTok and Instagram mostly happen on your phone, where the browser extension can't see. Their data download fills that gap. Request it in the app, then drop the file here.

TikTok

  1. Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Account
  2. Download your data → choose JSON → Request data
  3. When it's ready (usually hours), download it from the Download data tab

Gives us: videos your For You feed played, and your likes.

Instagram

  1. Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions
  2. Download your information → Some of your information → Your activity + Ads information
  3. Format JSON, date range All time → download when notified

Gives us: accounts whose posts you viewed, and posts you liked or saved.
